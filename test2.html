Semi truck training yard

Learn to backup semi trailers of different types

Hook up different trailers and practice parking. Drive out of the yard on curved roads. Practice staying in your lane on the cone course in the yard.

How to play

Driving

  • ↑ or W  speed up
  • ↓ or S  slow down, then reverse
  • ← → or A D  turn the steering wheel. Hold the key and the wheel turns steadily, up to 180° each way — quickest when backing up, quick at yard and corner speeds, slower and finer on the open road. It stays where you leave it — press the other arrow to bring it back. The bar at the top shows where the wheel is. Click the wheel in cab view to straighten it
  • B  brake
  • Right Shift shift up · Left Shift shift down (or press 1 2 3 to pick a gear; it drops to 1st when you stop) — 3 gears: 1st up to 25 km/h, 2nd up to 55, 3rd up to 90. A gear that's too high pulls weakly from slow, and a gear that's too low holds you back
  • Yard speed limit is 20 km/h forward and 5 km/h in reverse
  • Outside the cab, drag to look around and scroll to zoom
  • The west gate of the yard leads onto a winding hill route that loops back in through the north gate. Hills pull the truck back going up and push it along going down

Backing up

  • While reversing, the side camera monitors swing to follow the back corners of the trailer
  • Turn the wheel the opposite way you want the trailer to go — wheel right sends the trailer left
  • Small steering moves and slow speed: if it gets away from you, pull ahead and start again
  • To hook up, back straight under the trailer's kingpin, stop, then press Hook up

Cone course

  • A lane marked with cones loops round the open part of the yard, to the right as you come in the gate — drive it like a highway lane, both ways
  • It widens through the bends: swing the nose wide so the trailer doesn't cut in over the inside cones
  • Gaps in the cones keep the drives to the west gate, the north gate and the stalls clear
  • Cones hit at the top counts every cone the truck or trailer knocks over. Reset cones in the Menu stands them back up and sets the count to 0

Top bar

  • Menu opens the menu with the buttons below
  • km/h your speed, with a note when reversing or in the yard
  • Steering bar shows where the wheel is — centred means straight
  • Gear the gear you're in
  • Cones hit cones knocked over on the cone course
  • Hook up / Unhook couples or drops the trailer — the truck must be stopped. Dropped trailers stay where you leave them
  • Hook up / Unhook pup shows with either Super B to drop or pick up the rear 28 ft trailer

Menu

  • Drive cruise forward on its own until you touch a key
  • Lights headlights and marker lights
  • Night switches between day and night
  • Next view steps through outside camera angles
  • Cab view sit in the driver's seat
  • Autopilot line up in a lane on the hill route or side road and it steers and sets the speed for you, using the same wheel. Any driving key takes back control
  • Download drive log saves a record of the last drive (yours and the autopilot's) as a CSV file
  • Trailer start over at the gate with the van, flatdeck, Super B, Super B van or no trailer
  • Park all in stalls puts every trailer back in its yard stall
  • Reset cones stands the cones back up and sets the count to 0
  • Cab color paints the truck
